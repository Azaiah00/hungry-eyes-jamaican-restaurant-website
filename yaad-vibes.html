Yaad Vibes Friday

Every Friday, Hungry Eyes turns up. Self-serve karaoke at 6, a live DJ at 8, and good food all night long in Short Pump.

How the night runs

Two vibes, one night

Grab the mic first, then let the DJ take over. Dinner, drinks and dancing without changing venues.

  1. 6 PM

    Self-serve karaoke

    Pick your song, get in the queue and take the mic. R&B, reggae, hip hop, afrobeats, old school: bring your crew and your heart-out song.

  2. 8 PM

    Live DJ

    Rotating DJs take over the decks with reggae, dancehall, soca and afrobeats, plus R&B and hip hop, until close.

  3. $5

    Drink specials

    $5 drink specials all night, with Friday food specials like jerk wings and rasta pasta.

Friday FAQ

Before you pull up

What night is karaoke at Hungry Eyes?

Friday. Self-serve karaoke starts at 6 PM at Hungry Eyes (Yaad Vibes Bar & Grill) in Short Pump.

What time does the live DJ start?

The live DJ starts at 8 PM on Friday nights and plays until close.

What kind of music is played?

The DJs spin reggae, dancehall, soca and afrobeats, with R&B and hip hop in the mix. Karaoke song picks run from R&B and reggae to hip hop, afrobeats and old school.

Are there drink specials on Fridays?

Yes. Friday nights come with $5 drink specials, plus food specials like jerk wings and rasta pasta.

Can I order food during Yaad Vibes?

Yes. The kitchen serves the full Jamaican menu, and Friday brings specials like jerk wings and rasta pasta.
