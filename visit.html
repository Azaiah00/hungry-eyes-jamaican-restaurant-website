Visiting FAQ

Before you head over

Is Hungry Eyes open on Mondays?

Yes. Hungry Eyes is now open on Mondays from 11:30 AM to 7 PM.

Where do I park?

Park in the shared shopping-center lots around Towne Center West Blvd in the West Broad Village area, then look for the Hungry Eyes sign over Suite 707.

Can I order ahead for pickup?

Yes. Call (804) 664-2283 with your order and pick it up at the counter.

Do you deliver?

Delivery is available on DoorDash.

Are you open on holidays?

Holiday hours can change. Hungry Eyes posts closures and special hours on Instagram @hungryeyesrva.
